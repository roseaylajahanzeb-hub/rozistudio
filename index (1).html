05 / The little details

Good to
know.

A few helpful notes before we make something lovely together.

Do I need to have a domain?

Not to get started. A domain is your website address. We can help you choose one; registration and renewal are paid separately by you.

Are the websites mobile-friendly?

Yes. The layouts are designed to adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

Can you add online payments or a shop?

Ecommerce and payment integrations are not included in these packages. They can be scoped and quoted separately.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, service or product information, contact details and any photos or brand assets you want us to use.
